Blocs are how we add logic to the UI and also bridge the gap between the UI and the lower parts of your app, 
blocs should be used sparingly and should cover only a single topic. A good rule to check if if your bloc is too bloated
is to say this: "my bloc is responsible for (the responsiblity of your bloc)" if you didn't use the word 'and',
that means you have a good single responsiblity bloc, if you need to add 'and',
you might want to add another bloc to take some responsiblity from your bloated bloc.

Usually you'd also name your bloc accroding to its function, example:

A bloc that is responsible for your theme management -> theme_bloc;
A bloc that is responsible for your camera/video management -> camera_bloc.

For information on the basics of the bloc library:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w6XWjpBK4W8&list=PLptHs0ZDJKt_T-oNj_6Q98v-tBnVf-S_o

Do note that although the bloc library also offers the use of cubits, we don't really use them.
